Profile

Economics student focused on empirical macroeconomics

I am an M.Sc. student in Economics at KU Leuven with research interests in macroeconometrics, time-series econometrics, macroeconomic forecasting, and monetary economics. I am particularly interested in Inflation-at-Risk, Growth-at-Risk, Bayesian VARs, and DSGE models.

My experience combines empirical research, microeconometric replication, data engineering, reproducible workflows, statistical visualization, and regional geospatial analysis. I work primarily with Stata, R, MATLAB/Dynare, and LaTeX.
